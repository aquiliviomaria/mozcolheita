MOZCOLHEITA — TECHNOLOGY MODULES

Composition
FarmGuide owns FarmTechnology under its pausable world node.
FarmTechnology composes IrrigationDrone and ModernFences, and installs
TractorEquipment in the streamed vehicle.truck view. Equipment is recreated
when the vehicle streams back in. Authoritative state stays in PlayerData.guide.

Data
FarmData owns tilled/seeded tiles and moisture. irrigate(grid, litres) returns
absorbed litres, never invents tiles and rejects ripe crops. At >=80 moisture,
harvest_crop adds one produce unit. Base growth remains 240 game minutes.
Existing exact farm JSON already serializes moisture/nutrients.
PlayerData.guide.machinery stores selected equipment, attached flag, tank and
three world-space dock locations. Lowered/active reset on load for safe resumption.
PlayerData.guide.drone stores water and battery; flight itself is transient.
PlayerData.guide.modern_fences stores pairs of three-component world endpoints.
SaveManager already serializes this dictionary; no slot format migration required.
The public name is MozColheita. Custom user directory retains the original
~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/OpenAcre path for existing saves.

Presentation
EquipmentVisuals builds visual-only three-point attachments; no extra rigid
constraint is added to the original GEVP drivetrain. The old physical hitch and
trailer remain functional through Z/X/C/G. Mounting modern equipment rejects an
occupied legacy three-point hitch. P/V/B are reserved for modern equipment.
SoilFurrows binds/rebinds FarmData.tile_updated and batches nearby ridges in two
MultiMeshes (dry/wet); it reconstructs after a load and supplements Terrain3D
runtime painting. Crop growth stages retain cached meshes and batch rendering.
ModularHuman provides the permanent woven straw hat, articulated walk/work and
seated poses; Vehicle3D refresh_driver_visual shows a matching clone in the cab,
while the original on-foot physics body remains disabled through the seat controller.
ModernFences validates endpoints, protects access, creates static collision and
shares material caches. Its ghost mesh rebuilds only when its snapped position changes.
RuralVegetation shares one acacia mesh across 18 static trunks so the saw can
identify and remove individual trees. Felled IDs restore through the load signal.
Continuous human contours and curved mouldboards are indexed before merging
with primitive meshes; mixing unindexed and indexed surfaces otherwise drops faces.

Input and camera
FarmGuide.blocks_gameplay blocks normal controls during remote drone flight.
IrrigationDrone owns its flight camera, returns the Terrain3D camera on landing,
consumes battery and spray water, and auto-returns at zero battery or player death.
Fence mode preserves walking/look controls but intercepts farm actions/clicks.
Input handling in Vehicle3D offers keys to TractorEquipment before legacy hitches.
All HUD descriptions derive from current technology state rather than hardcoded
instructions that would stay visible when controls no longer apply.

Signals
FarmData.tile_updated drives soil/crop presentation, including moisture refresh.
TractorEquipment.equipment_changed, IrrigationDrone.flight_changed and
ModernFences.fence_changed are available to future UI/audio/achievement modules.
Existing EventBus.farm_hud_updated drives the current unified HUD snapshots.

Neighbour parcels
Three owners have three main farms and three satellite parcels. Twelve articulated
workers/drivers use closed routes on those parcels. Production/gifts/orders keep
using the existing owner simulation; their display fields do not mutate player soil.

Production and employment
FarmGuide composes ProductionMissions, WorkerEmployment and HandToolActions.
FarmWorker delegates hired workers to WorkerEmployment before its neighbour route.
Contracts use stable owner/route IDs and persist assigned plot, paid wages and tasks.
A worker reserves one of 24 cells in its assigned plot, travels, performs three
seconds of work, then charges 8 MT only after a successful mutation through FarmData.
Planting consumes seeds; harvesting uses ProduceInventory and returns two seeds.
No funds means waiting. The interaction dialogue reassigns or dismisses staff.
ProductionMissions records action totals and permanent reward claims; irrigation
counts only crossings of 80% moisture. Direct save hydration never adds progress.
HandToolActions owns wood/greenhouse data and rebuilds geometry after loading.
Tree IDs are compared as integers because JSON restores number arrays as floats.
RuralAmbience loops an original synthesised rural soundscape with quieter nights.

Rotation
FarmTileData stores last_family and rotation_streak alongside existing nutrients.
FarmData.harvest_crop applies a same-family penalty, a different-family bonus
when soil health allows, and a low-nutrient penalty, bounded to one product minimum.
Harvest changes soil health and preserves family history across replanting.
Exact tile JSON version 2 adds optional history fields; version 1 rows still load.
The contextual HUD shows soil health and a crop recommendation.

HUD and tools
VitalIcon draws vector status rings with small numbers instead of long life bars.
The right column hides its local map during vehicle/drone operation to fit controls.
CouplingIndicator is a shared green distance-gated 3D beacon for all three machines
and the drone. The drone camera accounts for its larger 3.3 m rotor footprint.
Mouse wheel and 7/8/9 select hoe/saw/hammer on foot; Ctrl+wheel retains camera zoom.
1-6 keep crop selection, and 7-9 select machinery when driving. The player's Tool
inventory and FarmGuide selection stay in sync. Hand tools attach to the wrist.

seven-head adult proportions, four skin tones and mild stature/build variation.

coupling availability, persistent drone controls and employee timer/progress fixes.
